---
name: VIVID City demo scope
description: Product scope and privacy constraints for the VIVID City real-estate explorer.
---

VIVID City is an Arabic-first real-estate exploration demo. The initial visitor flow covers the project, buildings/floors, unit details, shareable links/QR codes, and visit requests. AR/VR are deferred until approved architectural assets are available.

The current New Cairo location, inventory, prices, completion date, artwork, and floor-plan illustrations are sample content. They must not be represented as verified development information.

Visit requests contain personal contact details. Do not add public read access for leads. A sales dashboard or other staff access must include authentication and authorization so ordinary sign-ups cannot view customer details.

**Why:** The project started without client-provided architectural models or confirmed live listing data, and lead records contain personal information.

**How to apply:** Preserve the demo labels while using seed content. Before treating the app as a live sales channel, replace the sample data/assets and configure role-restricted access for sales staff.
